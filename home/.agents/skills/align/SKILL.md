---
name: align
description: Align on a task's goal, constraints, and major choices when the user asks to be interviewed, grilled, or to clarify an idea before implementation.
---

Build shared understanding of the problem, success criteria, and consequential tradeoffs. Consider whether an existing feature or simpler solution covers the need.

Find facts in the repo yourself. Ask a small round of questions whose answers could materially change the outcome, scope, or technology choice; give your recommendation and its tradeoff with each. Let answers guide follow-up questions rather than exhausting every possible decision.

Stop when the goal, important constraints, and major choices are clear enough to begin. Leave reversible implementation details to discovery. If a choice needs evidence, propose the smallest useful experiment.

Summarize the agreed outcome and how to verify it. Distinguish fixed requirements, preferences, and open assumptions; preserve the reasons behind important choices. Do not promote a suggestion into a requirement without agreement.

An interview alone does not authorize implementation. If implementation is already requested, continue once material questions are resolved.
